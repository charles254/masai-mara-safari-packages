Hydrological Topography & River Crossing Mechanics

The Mara and Talek rivers represent formidable ecological barriers along the northward migration route. Herds navigate steep earthen escarpments and turbulent currents where historical crossing points (cul-de-sacs) dictate herd movements. Understanding river hydraulics and bank slope angles explains crossing duration and predator success rates.

  • Key historic crossing locations: Main Crossing, Cul-de-Sac, Look-Out, and Serena Crossing points
  • Pre-crossing dusting displays and herd pressure buildup lasting from several hours to multiple days
  • Nile crocodile (Crocodylus niloticus) territorial ambush zones concentrated around deep river pools
  • Post-crossing dispersal across the Mara Triangle and northern conservancy savanna grazing pastures
