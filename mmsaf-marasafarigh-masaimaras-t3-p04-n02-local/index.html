Predator Territory Mapping & Behavioral Identification

The Masai Mara hosts dense populations of apex predators whose survival depends on territorial dominance. Experienced guides recognize individual lion prides (such as the Marsh Pride and Ridge Pride) by facial whisker patterns, and identify territorial leopards by distinctive facial spot configurations.
